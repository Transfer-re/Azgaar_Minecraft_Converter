Conquest Ground Palettes

Every biome's surface as it will be built: layer blocks (layers=1..8) over full blocks, drawn from the real Conquest and ACRP textures with Continuity's repeat and random variants. The terrain below is the same synthetic patch for every biome (a sea on the left, a river, a hill with a steep east face), so biomes can be compared side by side.

Read this first

  • Every skin is a layer block. Sands, gravels and forest floors use conquest:*_layer; soils, muds and all rock use conquest:*_slab, which in Conquest also carries layers=1..8 (set type=bottom). The layer's sides show the same texture as its top, so the soil block beneath is only visible on steps taller than the layer.
  • Tints are half the look. Grass, clover, taiga grass, every "mossy"/"overgrown" block and small stones are greyscale and take the biome grass colour. Each palette sets its own grass/foliage colour.
  • With ACRP loaded, custom biome ids lose their tints. Polytone maps biome to colormap column with getOrDefault(biome, 0), so any biome id ACRP does not list gets column 0 (#b7c26c) whatever the biome JSON says. Switch "Tints" to "ACRP fallback" to see it. Fix options are in docs/conquest/palettes.md.
  • Not modelled here: Conquest's grass-fringe overlay (it draws grass edges onto neighbouring dirt, sand, gravel and small stones) and connected textures. In game, grass-to-sand edges will look softer than they do here. Plants, trees and props are not drawn.
  • Avoided on purpose: ACRP draws "Do not use!" on dry_mud and coarse_sandy_soil. bright_glacier_ice_slab has no layers property. Columnar and tuff stones read as masonry from above.

How a column is chosen

  1. Under water: the bed ramp by water depth picks the mix.
  2. Otherwise the sub-biome comes from warped cellular noise at the palette's patch scale, weighted by the sub-biome weights.
  3. Ramps apply in a fixed order (altitude, coast, bank, slope) and a matching band replaces the mix, so slope beats shore and shore beats altitude. Each variable gets noise of amplitude feather added first, so band edges dither.
  4. The material is drawn from the mix with the same cellular noise at clump scale, plus 15% per-block speckle.
  5. Height h becomes H8 = round(h·8) eighths: full blocks up to floor((H8−1)/8), then the material's layer block with layers = H8 − 8·yTop.
  6. Under a skin (grass, floor, moss): soilDepth·(1 − slope/1.5) blocks of soil, then stone. Under anything else: the material's own full block for that depth, then stone.

Slope is rise per block (1.0 = 45°). Coast and bank are horizontal distances in blocks to sea or river water. Altitude is in Azgaar height units (20 = sea level, 100 = the map's highest); the slider sets what sea level maps to, so it can push the patch up through the snow and lichen bands.
